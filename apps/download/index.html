First-time setup

  1. Run the downloaded installer, then open Music Control.
  2. Sign in with your Music Control administrator password to approve this computer.
  3. Choose a music folder. Downloads are kept in its Music Control subfolder.
  4. Use Music Setup to download required walk-up and situation-cue tracks before leaving Wi-Fi.
